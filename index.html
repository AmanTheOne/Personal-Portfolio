Case study 01

Learning that talks back.

Enterprise knowledge sat scattered across SharePoint, Udemy, email threads and local laptops. When someone needed an answer mid-task on Teams, they asked a colleague who pointed to another colleague. Guru puts a source-backed answer, a quiz or a role-play one question away, grounded in the company's own documents.

Retrieval-augmented

Every answer shows its sources.

Content is chunked, embedded and indexed in a secure vector store. Semantic retrieval pulls the most relevant chunks into the prompt, so answers cite verified company content.

Retention

Adaptive quizzes, not completions.

Retrieval practice with a timer and feedback. Guru tests what the learner is weak on and comes back to it, so a finished course isn't mistaken for a learned skill.

Role-play

Practice scenarios generated on the fly.

"Jump into a practice scenario and put your skills to a test." Guru builds a situation from context, plays the other side and debriefs.

Multimodal

Talk, type or tap.

Voice conversation for role-play and quick questions, with the same grounding and citation rules as text.

Flow of work

Inside Slack, Teams and Chrome.

A busy person opens Guru because they have a question right now, in the tool they're already in. No portal detour.

1,000+
Gurus created by customers within 10 days of launch

Admins and L&D teams build a named Guru by feeding it SOPs, decks, videos and web sources. Within a quarter every pro-tier customer had Gurus in daily use.

22%
reduction in hallucination rate

Via a custom AI evals framework: golden question sets, grounding checks, HITL review of failures and regression runs on every prompt or retrieval change.

When the corpus runs out

Out-of-corpus but learning-relevant questions are answered and clearly labelled as broader inference, never dressed up as company fact.

Configurable coaching style

From supportive mentor to straight-talking strategist. Context memory across sessions makes follow-ups natural.

Adopted far beyond the brief

Onboarding, compliance coaching, sales enablement, leadership development. One customer built a Guru for their child's school syllabus.

Case study 02

Ask your team's data a question.

Managers never opened the analytics dashboard. They didn't know which chart answered "who on my team hasn't done the compliance pathway?" Line Manager View lets them ask in plain English. The system translates the question into database queries and returns the answer as a table or chart, inside the manager's own team page.

60%+
of active managers adopted it in the first quarter

Earned, not mandated. Managers came back because the answer arrived faster than asking L&D for a report.

38%
increase in manager engagement

Measured as managers taking an action on a team member's learning within a week of asking a question, not as logins.

How it works

Natural language in. A schema-grounded text-to-SQL layer with guardrails (row-level permissions, read-only, allow-listed tables) generates the query. Results render as a table or chart, with the generated logic available for the sceptical manager who wants to check the working.

Case study 04

Static QR codes are worthless.
Dynamic ones are unbreakable.

During and after COVID, counterfeit medicines flooded the market. Copy one static QR code and you can print it on ten thousand fakes. psVerify gives every unit its own encryption-generated code and serialised URL. A second scan on the same code flags it as over-verified and warns the patient on the spot.

20 lakh packs a day, each with a code that cannot be copied.

Variable data printing on the packaging line gives every unit an encryption-generated identity. Not a sticker. Not a batch barcode. A serialised identity baked in at manufacture.

Drugs (Eighth Amendment) Rules, 2022

First provider to 100% client compliance.

India mandated QR codes on its 300 highest-selling medicine brands. I tracked the rule from draft stage, ran customer awareness and lead engagement personally, and shipped MVPs with Abbott and Sun Pharma. PharmaSecure was the first provider to deliver full compliance for every client, well ahead of the government deadline.

8B+
serialised packs

Across 150+ clients and 600+ packaging lines.

150M+
consumer verifications

Every scan logged. Every second scan on the same code flagged.

Top 3
Indian pharma companies adopted

Including Sun Pharma and Cipla.

Verification was just the beginning.

A scan is a moment of full attention. The post-scan page became a direct-to-patient engagement surface: dosage guidance, chronic-care programmes, brand content. The anti-counterfeit tool turned into a new recurring revenue stream, and a real-time map of where medicines move and where suspicious activity clusters.

Case study 05

Self-enrol. Track daily.
Earn real rewards.

psConnect 1.0 worked: 20,000 patients, 10,000 doctors, 800 field reps. But every enrolment ran through a missed call and a call-back, and every new patient meant a bigger ops team. I named the ceiling and led the rebuild as a self-serve iOS and Android platform with workflow automation and a configurable rewards engine.

12×
patient base, to 253K+

Self-enrolment against a unique psID at the clinic. The card, the doctor portal and the field rep stayed. The call centre went.

+30%
adoption, engagement doubled

Daily medicine logging with streaks and points, condition-specific education, reminders, counsellor chat.

92%+
medication adherence

Across six therapeutic areas including diabetes and oncology, measured per patient in real time.

From care tool to commercial intelligence.

Real-time adherence by individual, early at-risk detection before a patient lapses, drop-off reason tagging, refill patterns for sales forecasting and campaign ROI. Doctors see their own patients in a dedicated portal; managers see field performance in another.

iOS & AndroidGamificationRewards engineB2B configuration layerPush notificationsMultilingualReal-time analytics

Why rebuild, not patch

Linear cost growth, inconsistent experience, no self-serve, aggregate-only insight, rigid channels. Each crack compounded with scale. The hard call was how to rebuild without losing the brand equity and doctor trust that made 1.0 work.

Case study 06

Every pack gets an identity.
Every movement leaves a record.

Products shipped from A to B and nobody could prove they arrived. Diversions, phantom deliveries, failed inspections. psID Trail assigns an encrypted identity to every pack at manufacture and captures each scan-in and scan-out, from factory dispatch to the hospital or pharmacy that handed it to a patient.

Add traceability without adding a single step to the line.

Manufacturers already had printing vendors and packaging lines. We ingested whatever format the vendor produced, normalised it and made it actionable. Zero-friction integration became the single biggest adoption driver.

2L+
packs scanned in and out every day

SSCC, 2D barcodes and data matrices normalised across country-specific regimes.

From compliance cost centre to commercial intelligence.

Time-to-market by region, distributor-level dispatch and receipt, verification rates at each node, early diversion detection. Role-based access lets manufacturers, distributors, regulators and ministries see exactly what their authority grants. A compliance log became a new recurring revenue stream.

About

Logins prove a platform is busy. I want to know whether someone became more capable.

I'm Aman, a product manager in Mumbai. I've spent seven years on products where the person who pays is not the person who uses: L&D leaders and busy employees, pharma companies and patients, regulators and manufacturers. The product only works when both sides win.

I care about the metric that survives a sceptical CFO. Completions and hours prove activity. A pack verified, a dose taken, a manager who acted on an answer: that's impact.

Right now I'm going deep on agentic systems, evaluation frameworks and the LLM tooling that turns a demo into something that survives Monday morning.
